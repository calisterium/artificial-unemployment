Our Story

It all started in a garage. Then it moved to a coworking space, then to an office, then to a tower, and now it is back in a garage, which we call our Innovation Lab. Along the way we have grown from 2 people to more than 40,000 people in 60 countries, and back to 2,000 people after the efficiency program. But our spirit has never changed.

Today, Generic Inc. serves 9 out of 10 companies that have ever existed. Our clients include leaders in technology, finance, healthcare, retail, consumer goods, energy, manufacturing, logistics, education, government, non-profits and companies that are not sure what industry they are in. We help them do what they do, better, faster and more generically.

We believe that people are our greatest asset. We also believe in the transformative power of technology to reduce our dependence on our greatest asset. We do not see a contradiction in this. We see an opportunity.
